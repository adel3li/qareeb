# StatusBanner

A quiet line on `surface-sunken` that confirms something already happened: today's dua was lived.

**Use** on the DuaCard after completion, in place of the primary button: `circle-check` in `success`, then «دعوتَ الله اليوم بهذا الدعاء» in `body-sm` 500, `text`; `radius-control`; `role="status"`. Under it, a secondary Button «أعِد التأمل».

**The consumer provides** the sentence and the icon.

**Don't** celebrate: no colour fill, no animation, no count of days, no "well done". The banner states a fact and steps back.
