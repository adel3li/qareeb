# Qur'an du'a coverage audit

An exhaustive pass over all 6236 verses (24 November 2026): every passage addressed to Allah that asks for something or seeks refuge, classified against the cards. References and descriptions only; no Qur'an text. Categories: A covered by a card; B partly covered; C missing and includable; D excluded by the content rules (disbelievers, hypocrites, people of the Fire, Iblis; praise or a statement without a request; commands without wording); E du'a against people; F specific to one person or situation; G duplicate.

After the audit, the B and C passages were handled: `dua_0044` widened to Maryam 4–6; `dua_0187`–`dua_0192` added (al-Nahl 98–99, al-A'raf 200–201, al-Kahf 23–24, al-Zumar 46, Yusuf 23, al-Shu'ara' 87–89). Left for the owner: Ghafir 56, al-A'raf 149, al-Qalam 29–32 (low confidence).
